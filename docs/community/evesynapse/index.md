# EveSynapse

EveSynapse is a free, self-hostable all-in-one companion for EVE Online. No ads. No trackers. No donation pop-ups.

It manages many characters under one account from a customizable widget home, and covers skills, industry, market, mail, assets, and intel in one place. It is open source, written in Go, and you can run it yourself with the installer or a Docker image.

## Features

- **Multi-character home** — a customizable widget overview for many characters under one account, with skills, training queues, industry jobs, mail, wallet, and contracts at a glance.
- **Industry planner** — full build trees with material and time, build-or-buy per line, and job scheduling across characters. Build costs come from your own swept market orders first, then your own stored estimates.
- **Market tools** — live market browser, price history, appraisals, and order health checks. Prices are computed from your own data, never from a third-party guide average.
- **Killboard and intel** — pilot activity profiles, fleet-type inference, and a live kill feed, with kill values priced from your own prices.
- **Universe map** — a 2D schematic map built for intel work: region and constellation recognition, sovereignty, a jump planner with range spheres, and an optional 3D view.
- **Fitting** — a fitting tool with per-module faction scaling and validation against the game's own rules.
- **Self-hosted** — install on your own server with the setup script or Docker. Your data stays yours.
