## About

Premier NPC killboard, ratting analytics, and PvE tools for EVE Online. Log combat and bounty tick data, track overall profits, analyze your ratting stats, and climb the leaderboards.

We also offer a variety of comprehensive pilot guides, pilot tools, and industry tools. 

## Key Features

nKillboard includes:

- **Killboard Stats**: shows your ratting stats and analytics
- **Leaderboard**: shows your place in the global leaderboard among other pilots.
- **Enhanced Market Browser**: allows you to browse any item across the whole of New Eden and filter based on jumps and systems.
- **Guides & Resources**: access variety of guides and resources for newbies and vets.
- **Universe Map**: view the New Eden map based on security, sovereignty, assets, pvp kills, gate camps, ratting and system traffic.
- **Wallet Journal**: view your wallet activity & history
- **Personal Assets**: view your personal assets across New Eden and get quick appraisal based on Jita Prices.
- **Skills & Queue**: View your acquired and queued skills in one place.
- **EVEMail Inbox**: read, write, send and delete your EVEMails.
- **Industry Jobs**: view your current active industry jobs, blueprints and PI.
- **Industry Calculator**: use more advanced version of the industry calculator with an improved UI, enhanced shareability for invoices, and shareable links.

For more visit [nKillboard](https://nkillboard.com)
